About SmartShop

Who We Are

SmartShop is an online store project designed to help students and young adults explore affordable electronics and computer accessories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make technology easier to explore by providing a simple, organized, and user-friendly shopping experience.

What We Offer

Our product catalog features electronics, accessories, and technology products. We also provide interactive tools that demonstrate how JavaScript can help users make calculations.

About This Project

This website is a course prototype built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It demonstrates website design, navigation, product presentation, and interactive applications.
